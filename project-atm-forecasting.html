ATM Data Time Series Forecasting

Forecasting 14 days of ATM transactions using time series analysis.

Time seriesARIMAExponential smoothingNeural networks

Exploration and analysis

  • Explored and analysed time series data to identify inherent patterns in real-world data.
  • Documented findings in a technical report with informative graphs.

Patterns identified

  • Revealed an additive trend and multiplicative seasonality in the dataset.
  • Discovered an additional weekly seasonality through rigorous analysis.
  • Used statistical tests to supplement visual analysis and resolve discrepancies.
  • Examined autocorrelation (ACF) and partial autocorrelation (PACF) plots.
  • Summarised patterns across all series and discussed what the commonalities and differences mean for automated forecasting.

Forecasting models

  • Built and evaluated seven models, including exponential smoothing, ARIMA, linear regression and neural networks.
  • Assessed performance with RMSE, MAE, MAPE, AIC and BIC, alongside time series cross-validation.
  • Compared every model against Naïve and Seasonal Naïve baselines to find the best fit for ATM withdrawal data.
