AI Lead Management Orchestration

Automates lead qualification and prioritisation using AI, reducing manual effort and helping sales teams focus on the enquiries most likely to convert.

AI automationLead qualificationSales prioritisation

Business challenge

A company receives around 200 enquiries a day through its website. Sales staff spend time reading every enquiry, identifying serious buyers, checking whether each lead fits the business, and deciding who should follow up first.

Project approach

This system automates repetitive lead-processing work, uses AI where language understanding is useful, and sends the sales team a prioritised queue of qualified leads.

Business objective

Reduce manual lead processing, improve response prioritisation, and help the sales team focus on leads most likely to convert.
